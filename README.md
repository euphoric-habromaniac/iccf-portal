# ICCF Portal

A competency certification portal for Manav Rachna University. Students take assessments and earn certifications. Staff manage the question bank and read results by department.

The portal is in development. This repository is a public summary only. The source code, data and design files are private, and this page does not describe how the system works internally.

## What it does

- **Assessments for soft skills.** A bank of 15 human-centric skills, such as communication, empathy, ethics and resilience, scored alongside regular subject competencies.
- **Five question types.** Likert, MCQ, scenario MCQ, ranking, and written answers graded against a rubric with AI.
- **Scorecards.** Each student gets a score and level for every skill, a radar chart, and a growth view across attempts.
- **Certificates and reports.** Certificates, student transcripts, and assessment and department reports export as PDFs.
- **Four roles.** Students, department heads, the core team and system admins each get their own dashboard.
- **Question moderation.** New questions are reviewed and approved before students ever see them.
- **Exam-day safeguards.** A timer that survives a reload, answers kept safe through a refresh, and tab-switch monitoring.
- **Bulk onboarding.** Staff add whole classes of students and teachers from a spreadsheet.

## Built with

React 19, TypeScript, Vite, Tailwind CSS, Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Gemini, Recharts.
